Built for the moment you open your mouth

Three things we obsess over — and three things textbooks never give you.

💬

Answer, don't recite

Every line starts as a question someone really asks you. You answer it — out loud — not read it off a page.

不是背课文：先有人问你，你再开口答。

📍

Learn where it matters

Scenes are the places you'll actually be: a noodle shop, a taxi, a first-day office hello. No "the cat is on the table."

学你真会用上的场景：面馆、出租车、第一天见同事。

🎧

Master tones by ear

Tone Gym trains the hard part first: hearing 妈 / 麻 / 马 / 骂 apart, then saying them until they land.

声调先练耳朵：听得出，才说得出。
