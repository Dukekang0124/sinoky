Contact

Just say what's wrong

Sinoky is an independent project. There's no support team and no ticket system — but every message gets read, usually by the person who writes the code.

💬

Fastest: the feedback button inside the app

Open Sinoky and tap the round feedback icon. Going this way, your message arrives with the app version, browser type and country already attached — the three things I need most and that are easiest to forget.

In-app · no sign-in

What makes a report easy to act on

  • Your device and OS (e.g. Pixel 7 / Android 14, or iPhone 13 / iOS 18)
  • Which browser (Chrome, Safari, in-app, or the Android app)
  • What you tapped, and what happened instead
  • If it's a content problem: which line, and what's wrong (a screenshot is ideal)

Or just email

Two addresses with a clear split — no guessing which one to use.

Everything else goes here

Bugs, feature ideas, a line that's wrong, offers to help translate, press and teaching enquiries — all of it goes here.

Data and privacy go here

Ask me to delete the records tied to your device, question the privacy policy, or tell me a disclosure doesn't match what the product actually does. You can ask me to delete the two records tied to your device ID, and I will.

privacy@sinoky.app

What we store, for how long and how to delete it is all written up in the data section of “How it works” and in the privacy policy.

If you teach Chinese, run a school, or make content

These get answered first, because they're consistently the most useful: you can spot an unnatural line instantly.

🎓

Teachers: vet the lines

If a “casual” variant isn't actually common where you're from, or a usage only holds in one region, tell me. Accuracy matters far more than volume here.

🏫

Schools: can we use it in class?

It's free, and no student accounts are needed — which is convenient for a classroom, since there's no roster to manage. If you plan to use it with a large group, give me a heads-up so I can cope with the traffic.

🎙️

Creators: coverage, critiques, collaborations

Podcasts, channels, teaching communities — just email me. Criticism is genuinely welcome too: what this gets wrong, and where the tool you already use beats it.

For these, one word in the subject line is enough for me to sort it — teacher, school, podcast or translation.

What to expect from me

Being clear beats sounding polished, so there's no SLA here.

✅ What I will do

  • Read every message, criticism included
  • Prioritise content and data issues, because those affect everyone
  • Ship fixes into the release notes, which you'll see the next time you open the app
  • Act on deletion requests and confirm when it's done

⚠️ What I can't promise

  • I can't promise a response time — this is one person
  • I won't build every requested feature, and I'll say plainly why not
  • No human pronunciation-marking service — the app already scores automatically
  • No phone support and no live chat
