Questions people actually ask

Is it really free?
Yes. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no trial. Both the web app and the Android app are free. The AI features (Nono chat, AI feedback) have a daily cap — that cap exists to control server cost, not to push you into paying. The core loop — listen, speak, get scored, review — uses none of it.
Do I need an account?
No. No email, no password, no phone number. Open it and go. Your progress is stored on your own device; only if you want to continue on a second device does an anonymous device ID come into play. See your data.
Can I use it if I know nothing at all?
Yes — it's designed for it. Pinyin comes first everywhere: every character has pinyin above it, and tapping any character plays its sound. Day one of the 30-day path teaches 你好 and 多少钱.
Does it work on Android and iPhone?
Both. On Android you can install the APK directly. On iPhone / iPad, open it in Safari and “Add to Home Screen” — offline, one tap, no address bar, essentially indistinguishable from an installed app. Both paths are spelled out on the download page.
Does it work offline?
After one visit, yes: lines, pinyin, characters and audio are cached on the device, so it works on a plane. What needs the network is speech scoring and Nono AI — offline, those fall back gracefully instead of blocking you.
Are my recordings uploaded?
Only when you tap the mic: the audio goes to a speech-recognition service to be transcribed and scored, then it's discarded — the raw audio is not stored. It is never messaged, shared or used for training. You can also skip recording entirely and just listen and shadow. See the privacy policy.
Is this a textbook? A dictionary?
Neither. It doesn't teach a grammar system and it isn't a dictionary. It does one thing: get you to say the right line in a real situation, out loud — and then remember it.
